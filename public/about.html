OUR HISTRY

OUR HISTORY

Agro and Delish were two startups founded by entrepreneurs passionate about sustainability and technology. Agro focused on solving food distribution inefficiencies in rural areas, using data to connect small farmers with local markets. Delish, on the other hand, was an online platform for recipes and fresh food, helping consumers access personalized, fresh meals.

Initially, the two companies operated separately, but over time, they realized there was a natural synergy between them. Delish could benefit from Agro’s locally sourced products, while Agro gained access to urban consumers through Delish. In 2024, they decided to merge their efforts, creating a “farm-to-table” platform that supported both farmers and food lovers, while promoting sustainability in the food chain.

DELICIOUS FOOD FOR HEALTHY LIFE

Quality Food

Food quality at AgriDelish is centered on freshness, sustainability, and the use of locally sourced, organic ingredients. Each dish is crafted to highlight the natural flavors of seasonal produce while maintaining high nutritional value. The focus is on delivering meals that are both healthy and flavorful, with an emphasis on farm-to-table authenticity.

ORIGINALTITY OF AGRIDELISH

The dishes of AgriDelish are inspired by farm-to-table ingredients, focusing on seasonal produce and traditional recipes. They emphasize fresh, organic, and healthy meals, including locally sourced vegetables, meats, and dairy products. AgriDelish also offers modern fusion by combining local ingredients with global culinary techniques. Menus change seasonally to highlight the freshest agricultural products. The concept prioritizes sustainability, freshness, and health-conscious eating.
